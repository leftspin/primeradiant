**Blocking Findings**

1. **Blocker: Low-confidence/refusal outputs can still produce material downstream behavior.**  
   `ProductionExtractor` always emits `source_stance` for parsed content, even when `quality_for/1` marks the result `low_confidence`, and the contract only blocks structural claims, not other material arrays. Worse, `Decider.decide/3` checks `stale_input?` before low-confidence handling, so a low-confidence “background check” can become a `:stale` graph operation instead of `abstain/needs_more_evidence`.

2. **Blocker: The envelope adapter drops validated evidence spans.**  
   The extractor result validates real byte spans in `evidence_index`, but `EnvelopeAdapter` maps every adapted claim/entity/question/framing span to `%{start: 0, end: 0}`. Downstream consumers receive invalid span data even though T321 requires evidence-backed atoms and visible evidence spans.  
   Refs: [envelope_adapter.ex:58](/Users/mike/shared-workspace/primeradiant/storage-harness/lib/primeradiant/extraction/envelope_adapter.ex:58), [envelope_adapter.ex:77](/Users/mike/shared-workspace/primeradiant/storage-harness/lib/primeradiant/extraction/envelope_adapter.ex:77), [envelope_adapter.ex:91](/Users/mike/shared-workspace/primeradiant/storage-harness/lib/primeradiant/extraction/envelope_adapter.ex:91), [envelope_adapter.ex:101](/Users/mike/shared-workspace/primeradiant/storage-harness/lib/primeradiant/extraction/envelope_adapter.ex:101), [envelope_adapter.ex:164](/Users/mike/shared-workspace/primeradiant/storage-harness/lib/primeradiant/extraction/envelope_adapter.ex:164)

3. **Blocker: Opinion-column source type suppresses span-supported factual claims.**  
   `opinion_source?/2` marks every claim in an `opinion_column` as `modality: "opinion"`, and `structural_claim?/3` then prevents those claims from becoming structural candidates. The spec explicitly says opinion articles can contain factual claims and that span/modality decide treatment. The test corpus encodes the opposite by expecting no facts for an opinion item that directly says service is halted.  
   Refs: [production_extractor.ex:212](/Users/mike/shared-workspace/primeradiant/storage-harness/lib/primeradiant/extraction/production_extractor.ex:212), [production_extractor.ex:276](/Users/mike/shared-workspace/primeradiant/storage-harness/lib/primeradiant/extraction/production_extractor.ex:276), [production_extractor_quality_test.exs:400](/Users/mike/shared-workspace/primeradiant/storage-harness/test/primeradiant/production_extractor_quality_test.exs:400)

4. **Blocker: The eval corpus is still grammar-shaped, so it does not prove T321 production quality.**  
   The extractor primarily accepts `Subject predicate is value` / `predicate is value` patterns, and the quality corpus is built around that same convenience grammar rather than “messier real text with no grammar shortcuts.” This is exactly the boundary the spec says must not be treated as product truth.  
   Refs: [production_extractor.ex:137](/Users/mike/shared-workspace/primeradiant/storage-harness/lib/primeradiant/extraction/production_extractor.ex:137), [production_extractor_quality_test.exs:300](/Users/mike/shared-workspace/primeradiant/storage-harness/test/primeradiant/production_extractor_quality_test.exs:300), [production_extractor_quality_test.exs:580](/Users/mike/shared-workspace/primeradiant/storage-harness/test/primeradiant/production_extractor_quality_test.exs:580)

5. **Blocker: Downstream contradiction and identity gates ignore required extractor fields.**  
   `first_conflict/3` compares only fact key/value and compatible scope; it does not compare polarity, modality, attribution, or uncertainty before creating a conflict candidate. Identity mismatch handling is also limited to `venue`/`location`, missing actor, time scope, and affected-group near misses required by the spec.  
   Refs: [decider.ex:142](/Users/mike/shared-workspace/primeradiant/storage-harness/lib/primeradiant/story_identity/decider.ex:142), [decider.ex:193](/Users/mike/shared-workspace/primeradiant/storage-harness/lib/primeradiant/story_identity/decider.ex:193), [real_ingestion.ex:1001](/Users/mike/shared-workspace/primeradiant/storage-harness/lib/primeradiant/storage_harness/real_ingestion.ex:1001)

6. **Blocker: Graph-changing evidence refs are input-level labels, not extractor evidence spans.**  
   The decider emits `evidence_refs: [envelope.input_ref]`, and proposal/fact/conflict ops propagate that input ref rather than the claim’s span-backed `evidence_refs`. That means downstream graph commits cannot validate or preserve the exact extractor span that supported the fact.
